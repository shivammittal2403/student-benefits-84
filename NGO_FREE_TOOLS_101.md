# 101 additional free resources useful to NGOs

Research date: 2026-09-27. These are **101 distinct products/services** beyond the 27 NGO program rows in [NGO_OFFERS.md](NGO_OFFERS.md), for **128 NGO-relevant directory entries in total**. They are **not 128 NGO-only grants**. The majority are open-source tools usable by any organization. Their software license is free, but hosting, security maintenance, integrations, support, payment processing, hardware, or higher service tiers can cost money. `FREE / FREE-TIER` items are public services with their own quotas and terms. Official project or provider pages are linked; actual eligibility, license scope, availability in India and current pricing should be reconfirmed before deployment. Open-source does not guarantee a managed cloud plan is free. Avoid entering donor or beneficiary personal data until the deployment and retention controls are configured.
